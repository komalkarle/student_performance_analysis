### 6. Key Insights
-Students with higher previous scores generally perform better.
-Study hours show a positive relationship with exam scores.
-A few students have unusually low exam scores (outliers).
-Attendance is fairly evenly distributed.
-The dataset contains no missing values or duplicates.
### 7. Conclusion
This analysis suggests that previous academic performance and study habits are positively associated with exam scores. The dataset is clean and suitable for further predictive modeling. These findings can help educators identify factors that contribute to better student performance.
### 8. Recommendations
Encourage students to maintain consistent study habits.
Identify students with low previous scores and provide additional support.
Promote balanced internet usage and healthy sleep habits.
Track attendance regularly to monitor student engagement.
